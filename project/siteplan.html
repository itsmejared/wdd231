Site Plan: FSY Music & Variety Program

Site Name

FSY Music & Variety Program

This name reflects the goal of the website: to introduce, guide, and support youth, parents, and leaders in understanding the Music & Variety portion of the FSY experience.

Optional Domain: fsy-music-variety.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to clearly explain the FSY Music & Variety Program, including audition requirements, participation guidelines, standards, and expectations. The site will also provide examples and helpful resources. The goal is to help youth feel prepared, confident, and excited to participate.

Scenarios (Audience Questions)

  • What are the requirements and standards for participating in the Music & Variety program?
  • How do I submit an audition using the online form?
  • Where can I find examples of appropriate performances?

Color Scheme

The color palette reflects youthfulness, energy, and uplifting creativity:

  • FSY Blue – #005BBB (headings and accents)
  • Sunrise Yellow – #FFD447 (buttons and highlights)
  • Soft Light Gray – #F4F4F4 (background)
  • Deep Charcoal – #222222 (main text)

These colors are applied throughout this document as required by the rubric.

Typography

Headings: Montserrat — bold, modern, and energetic.

Body Text: Inter — clean, readable, ideal for digital content.
