Site Plan: FSY Music & Variety Program

Site Name

FSY Music & Variety Program

This name reflects the purpose of the website: to introduce, guide, and support youth, parents, and leaders in understanding the Music & Variety portion of the FSY experience.

Optional Domain: fsy-music-variety.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to clearly explain the FSY Music & Variety Program, including audition requirements, participation guidelines, expectations, and standards. The site will also provide examples and resource materials to help youth feel confident and prepared to participate.

Scenarios (Audience Questions)

  • What are the requirements and standards for participating in the Music & Variety program?
  • How do I submit an audition using the online form?
  • Where can I find examples of appropriate and uplifting performances?

Color Scheme

This project will use a modern and youthful four-color palette:

  • Navy Modern – #1B2A41 (header and footer background)
  • Fresh Mint – #2EC4B6 (buttons, highlights, accents)
  • Cloud – #F7F7F7 (main background)
  • Graphite – #1E1E1E (text color)

These colors provide clarity, contrast, and a modern look suitable for youth-focused content.

Typography

Headings: Montserrat — bold, modern, and energetic.

Body Text: Inter — clean, readable, ideal for longer paragraphs.
