Guidelines & Audition Information

The FSY Music & Variety Program is designed to invite the Spirit, strengthen testimonies, and provide opportunities for youth to share their talents in uplifting ways. Every performance should reflect gospel standards and support the overall purpose of FSY.

General Standards

  • Content should be faith-building, clean, and in harmony with the teachings of Jesus Christ.
  • Lyrics and spoken words must avoid inappropriate language, themes, or innuendo.
  • Costumes and clothing should be modest and appropriate for a Church setting.
  • Performances should typically be between 2–4 minutes in length.
  • Collaborative performances are encouraged when they help invite the Spirit.

Frequently Asked Questions

In some cases, secular music with a clearly uplifting message may be appropriate, but it should still support the purpose of FSY and invite the Spirit. Please avoid songs that are strongly associated with worldly themes, romance, or rebellion, even if the lyrics are edited. When in doubt, choose music that clearly focuses on the Savior, faith, gratitude, or other gospel-centered topics.

Please record the full version of your planned performance so that your FSY leaders can review timing, content, and overall tone. Simple phone recordings are perfectly acceptable. Your leaders are more interested in spiritual impact and preparation than in professional production quality.

Many youth feel nervous about performing, and that is completely normal. The Music & Variety Program is not a talent show focused on perfection; it is an opportunity to bless others. If your desire is to testify of Christ and uplift your peers, you are exactly the kind of participant we are hoping for.

If you feel ready to share your talents in an uplifting way, please submit your audition using the form below.
